The full catalogue

Nine lines. One standard of finish.

Emulsion paint is a water-based coating built on an acrylic resin binder — durable, washable, and easy to keep clean. Interior emulsions are tuned for stain resistance and a smooth finish; exterior emulsions are engineered to take on UV, rain and fungal growth. Everything below is built, packed and depot-stocked to that logic.
