About Diamond Paints · Est. 2019

Formulated on the factory floor, not in a boardroom.

Our story

Small batches, kept honest, scaled slowly.

Diamond Paints was established in 2019 with a single promise: make products a contractor doesn't have to think twice about. That meant starting narrow — wall putty and interior primer — and only adding a new line once the last one was consistent, batch after batch, depot after depot.

Today the range spans interior and exterior primers, the Shine and Apex emulsion families, a full Deluxe colour card, wall putty, POP, distemper and decorative luxe metallics — sold through sales depots across Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana. What we hold ourselves to

Four rules that don't bend for volume.

01

Consistency over speed

A batch that's off-shade gets held back, even if it delays dispatch. Contractors shouldn't have to blend buckets.

02

System compatibility

Our primers, putty and topcoats are formulated to work together, so warranty claims aren't a finger-pointing exercise.

03

Honest coverage claims

Pack sizes and coverage figures are tested, not aspirational — what's on the bucket is what you'll get on the wall.

04

Dealer partnership

Our depots exist to keep dealers stocked, not to compete with them — support first, always.
